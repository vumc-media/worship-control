Why Worship Control?

Built to help churches stay focused on worship—not technology.

Sunday mornings move quickly. Worship leaders should not have to return to the media booth to advance slides. Pastors should not have to interrupt rehearsal to update a stage display. Volunteers deserve tools that are simple, dependable, and shaped around ministry.

Built Around Ministry

Worship Control began with real needs inside a working local church, not as a classroom exercise or a generic remote-control concept.

Focused by Design

The goal is not to duplicate every ProPresenter window. It is to surface the controls leaders and production teams need most during a service.

Tested on Sundays

Features are being refined through live worship services, rehearsals, and real production workflows before public release.

What We're Building

A modern workspace for the moments that matter most.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know while Worship Control is being built.

Is Worship Control available now?

Not yet. Worship Control is still under active development and is not currently available for purchase or public download.

Will there be a free trial?

A fully functional 14-day evaluation period is planned before the commercial release so churches can test Worship Control in their own network and ministry environment.

Does Worship Control require NDI?

No. Worship Control does not require NDI. It is being designed to work comfortably alongside churches that already use NDI-based video production, PTZ cameras, Companion, live streaming, and multi-display systems.

Is Worship Control an NDI video switcher?

No. Worship Control focuses on presentation control and related ProPresenter workflows. A separate software project, LiveRoute Studio, is being developed specifically for NDI video switching and PTZ production workflows.

Is Worship Control affiliated with Renewed Vision?

No. Worship Control is an independent third-party application designed to communicate with ProPresenter using its documented APIs. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renewed Vision.

Which platforms are planned?

Worship Control is being developed as a desktop application for both macOS and Windows. Final system requirements will be published before release.
